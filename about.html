Our Story

"The Royal Bite" was founded in 2012 with a passion for creating unforgettable dining experiences. What started as a small family kitchen soon became a go-to spot for food lovers. Our chefs bring years of expertise, using only the finest ingredients to craft dishes that satisfy the soul. Every meal we serve is a blend of tradition, innovation, and love—because great food isn’t just about taste, it’s about the memories it creates."

Meet Our Head Chef

Chef Habib Korai: has over 20 years of experience in Italian and French cuisine. He has worked in Michelin-starred restaurent and now brings his expertise to The Royal Bite.
